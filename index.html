Calculation Methodology and Physical Constraints

Why is similarity with Mars ~50–66% and with Moon ~34–48%?

Unlike naive averaging, the algorithm applies a strict weighted Gaussian metric and interplanetary divergence coefficient for gravity (1.00 g / 0.38 g / 0.166 g), pressure (1013 mbar / 6.1 mbar / vacuum), and radiation background. Even an ideal Earth site (Atacama or Antarctica) remains only a partial analog of another planet.

"Climatology" vs "Live Weather" Separation

Current weather (Open-Meteo) is used exclusively to evaluate the suitability of the Earth site for rover or drone field tests today, without distorting the fundamental geological and climatic profile of the territory.

Source Transparency: When network is available, the application requests NASA POWER climatology, Macrostrat geologic map, and Open-Meteo weather in real-time. For Moon and Mars, orbital models calibrated against published LRO and MGS/MRO mission datasets are used (including reference regions: Shackleton crater, Mare Tranquillitatis, Marius Hills, South Pole-Aitken basin, Jezero and Gale craters, Hellas planitia, Olympus Mons, and Valles Marineris).
